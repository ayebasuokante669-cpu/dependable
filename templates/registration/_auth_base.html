{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} The split panel every signed-out screen sits in: sign in, sign up, and the four password-reset stages. One layout, so the login page and the reset page cannot drift apart. Shape: a branded gradient panel on one side, the form on the other. Which side the visual panel takes is the page's choice -- sign-in keeps it on the left, sign-up reverses it -- and that reversal is the whole trick behind the swap: navigating between the two moves the panel across the screen, which js/ui.js plays as a crossing when it knows you came from the other page (see `initAuthSwap`) and as a short settle otherwise. Below `lg` there is no split. The visual panel collapses to a short banner above the form, because a phone has room for one column and the form is the one that matters.
