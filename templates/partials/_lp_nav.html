{% comment %} The public site's sticky bar. The section links are real anchors. Smooth scrolling and the offset that keeps a heading clear of this bar are both declared in the stylesheet (`scroll-behavior` / `scroll-padding-top`), so they work with the keyboard, in a new tab, and with this page's JavaScript blocked. js/ui.js adds only two things on top: `.is-scrolled` once the page has left the hero, and the `aria-current` that marks the section being read. The bar starts transparent over the navy hero and becomes frosted glass over the page -- so everything on it has two colour states, handled by `.lp-nav` descendant rules in the stylesheet rather than by classes here. The one exception is the primary button, which is navy-on-white in both and needs no switch. Shown on the landing page only -- the auth screens have their own chrome. {% endcomment %}
