{# ===== Who we are =================================================== #}

Who we are

Running a school is hard enough without fees getting in the way. {{ product_name }} is school management software built for Nigerian schools, and it starts with the part that causes the most headaches: who owes what, and who has paid.

We replace the notebooks, the scattered spreadsheets and the weekend of adding up with one record everyone reads from. Set your fees once, by class and by term. Every balance, reminder and receipt then comes from the same figures, so the answer to “has this child paid?” takes a second.

Alongside fees, {{ product_name }} keeps your student roster and sends reminders straight to parents' phones. Parents don't need to install anything. Each person on your staff sees only what their role allows, and proprietors with more than one campus can see every branch side by side from a single login.

We built {{ product_name }} because good schools shouldn't lose time and goodwill to messy records. Bring the list you already keep, and we'll help you get set up. Nothing is charged until you say so.

{# ===== Why fees first =============================================== #}

Why we start with the fees

Most school software starts with timetables and report cards, and asks the bursary to wait. We went the other way round, because the question a school actually cannot answer on a Monday morning is who has paid.

Nothing is stored twice. A balance is never typed in and never cached, so re-pricing a class or reversing a payment moves the child's record, the bursar's chase list, the collections report and the reminder a parent receives — all of them, at once, with no recalculation step for anybody to forget.

{# ===== How we handle school data ==================================== #}

How we handle school data

A school's records belong to that school. Every row on the platform is owned by one school and one campus, and the separation is enforced in the data layer rather than left to each screen to remember — so one school cannot see another's students, fees, payments or messages, whatever anyone types into a URL.

What we hold, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to ask to see, correct or delete it — including under Nigeria's NDPA — is set out in full in the privacy policy.
