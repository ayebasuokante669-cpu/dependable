{% comment %} The platform owner's module switches for one school. Takes `school` and `modules` -- the rows from apps.core.modules.state_for_school: one per *toggleable* module, each knowing whether it is on and whether that is a decision somebody made or just the registry default. Why each switch is its own form: * it POSTs the module key *and* the state it wants, never "flip it". A toggle that flipped whatever it found would do the wrong thing the moment somebody double-submitted, or pressed the switch in a tab that had gone stale; * one form per row means no JavaScript, which means the switch works the same on the office desktop and on a phone with a bad connection; * a POST rather than a link, because this changes what a school is paying for and a GET that did that would be followed by every crawler and prefetcher. The always-on modules are listed below the switches without one. They are part of the product, `SchoolModule.set_state` refuses to record a decision about them, and showing them greyed-out with no control is more honest than leaving the reader to wonder where student records went. {% endcomment %}
