{% comment %} The hero's floating product cards. Not screenshots: real fragments of the interface, built from the same tokens, components and payment-status colours the app itself uses. A screenshot goes stale the week after it is taken and cannot follow the theme; this follows both for free, and is what the product genuinely looks like because it is made of the product. The figures are illustrative and generic on purpose -- no school's name, no child's name. They are marked `aria-hidden` and the hero's copy carries the message, so a screen reader is not read three tables of invented numbers. Each card carries its own `--float-delay` and `--float-distance` so the three drift out of step with one another; `[data-perf="lite"]` stops the drift entirely. {% endcomment %}
